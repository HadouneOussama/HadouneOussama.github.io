## About me

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at [École des Mines de Saint-Étienne](https://www.mines-stetienne.fr/), working on energy harvesting through NFC emitters integrated into textiles. Before that, I spent a year and a half at Télécom Paris within the [Energy4Climate (E4C)](https://e4c.ip-paris.fr/) initiative, where I built autonomous weather monitoring stations from scratch — full PCB design, solar harvesting, LoRa communication, STM32 with Zephyr RTOS, the whole stack.

My background is in embedded electronics and control systems — I did my PhD at Badji Mokhtar University in Annaba, Algeria, on real-time control of multi-axis systems. But over the years my interests have pulled me deeper into the intersection of hardware and AI: edge inference, acoustic emission monitoring, energy-aware sensing, and more recently NFC-based wireless power transfer.

I work across a few areas that don't always talk to each other — signal processing, embedded systems, and machine learning — and I find the bridges between them more interesting than any one field in isolation. A lot of my recent work involves getting AI to run on devices that weren't designed for it, or squeezing meaningful information out of signals that are hard to interpret.

Outside of research, I've been involved in teaching at different levels (L1 to M1), been part of national robotics and startup competitions in Algeria, and occasionally review papers for conferences and journals.

I'm always open to collaborations, especially at the intersection of embedded systems and applied ML. Feel free to reach out.

## Research interests

- Energy harvesting & wireless power transfer (NFC, RF)
- Acoustic emission monitoring for structural health
- Edge AI and embedded inference (STM32, RKNN, TFLite Micro)
- Low-power IoT systems and LoRaWAN sensor networks
- Signal processing and unsupervised learning for industrial data
