--- layout: archive title: "Portfolio" permalink: /portfolio/ author_profile: true --- {% include base_path %} A selection of research projects and hardware developments I've worked on. --- ## Autonomous Weather Monitoring Station *Télécom Paris — Energy4Climate project, 2024–2025* Full hardware design from scratch of an autonomous environmental sensing station. Custom PCB integrating solar energy harvesting, battery charging and monitoring (INA236), LoRa long-range communication, and an STM32 microcontroller running Zephyr RTOS. Designed for ultra-low-power outdoor deployment with no infrastructure dependency. **Keywords:** PCB design, KiCad, STM32, Zephyr RTOS, LoRa, solar harvesting, battery SoC estimation --- ## NFC Textile Charging System *Mines Saint-Étienne, 2026–present* Design and development of a wireless charging system where the power emitter is embedded directly into textile. Involves NFC antenna design at 13.56 MHz, energy transfer optimization, and integration constraints specific to wearable and flexible substrates. **Keywords:** NFC, energy harvesting, antenna design, wearable electronics --- ## GraphDARTS — Acoustic Emission Monitoring *Collaborative work with HBKU Qatar & FEMTO-ST* Contrastive graph metric learning framework for unsupervised neural architecture search applied to structural health monitoring. Evaluated on the ORION-AE benchmark dataset (bolt tightening, impact detection). Published in *Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing* (2026). **Keywords:** Graph neural networks, NAS, acoustic emission, structural health monitoring, contrastive learning --- ## LoRaWAN Environmental Sensor Node *Télécom Paris, 2024–2025* End-to-end development of a LoRaWAN sensor node (SHTC3 + MAX17055 on STM32WL) with Zephyr RTOS. Implemented delta encoding compression achieving ~79% payload reduction, Huffman coding comparison, TTN integration with custom decoders, duty cycle compliance, and FOTA with MCUboot. **Keywords:** LoRaWAN, STM32WL, Zephyr, FOTA, MCUboot, TTN, data compression --- ## Edge AI on Embedded Platforms *Ongoing* Deployment of deep learning models on resource-constrained hardware: STM32Cube.AI for STM32 targets, TFLite Micro, and RKNN NPU on Orange Pi 5 Plus. Work includes FP8/INT4 quantization, structured pruning, and latency/memory profiling for real-time inference at the edge. **Keywords:** Edge AI, STM32Cube.AI, TFLite Micro, RKNN, quantization, pruning --- ## Ball-on-Plate Control System (PhD work) *Badji Mokhtar University, 2019–2022* Real-time control of a ball-on-plate system using a 6-DOF parallel robot. Implemented and compared PID, Lead/Lag, Fuzzy-PID, and ANFIS controllers. Results published across IEEE SSD 2022, IJEEI journal, and EJOSAT conference proceedings. **Keywords:** ANFIS, Fuzzy-PID, parallel robot, real-time control, MATLAB/Simulink
